BETHEL SME FUNDINGS

Bethel Youth Capital Initiative

A youth-driven financial empowerment platform designed to support young entrepreneurs in Botswana through accessible short-term business funding solutions. We empower youths aged 18–25 with innovative, impactful, and visionary business ideas.

Micro-Loans

P500 to P2,000 for starting or scaling your business

8-Month Terms

Fair repayment period up to 8 months

15–30% Interest

Competitive rates under fair contractual agreements

Our Mission

Promote youth entrepreneurship
Encourage financial independence
Support sustainable business development
Empower communities through youth-led enterprises

Who Can Apply?

Youth aged 18–25 Students Non-students Strong business ideas Capital expansion needs

Loan Requirements

  • Valid National ID / Omang
  • Student ID (if applicable)
  • Completed Loan Application Form
  • Signed Loan Agreement
  • Detailed Business Proposal including: loan amount, purpose, growth strategy, estimated monthly revenue, and two-year vision
  • Optional collateral or reference (if required)
