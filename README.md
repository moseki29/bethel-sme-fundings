# BETHEL SME FUNDINGS

A micro-loan eligibility checker for the Bethel Youth Capital Initiative. This application helps young entrepreneurs (18-25 years) determine if they meet the requirements for P500-P2000 micro-loans.

## Features

- **Loan Information Display** - Clear presentation of micro-loan details (amount, interest rate, repayment period)
- **Applicant Information** - Collects name, age, and student status
- **Business Funding Purpose** - Options for establishing new or scaling existing businesses
- **Requirements Checklist** - Verifies all necessary documentation:
  - Valid National ID/Omang
  - Student ID (if applicable)
  - Completed Loan Application Form
  - Signed Loan Agreement
  - Business Proposal
  - Optional Physical Collateral/Reference
- **Real-time Validation** - Instant eligibility feedback with detailed error reporting

## How to Use

1. Fill in your personal information (name, age, student status)
2. Select your business funding purpose
3. Provide loan amount and expected monthly revenue
4. Check all applicable requirement documents
5. Click "CHECK LOAN ELIGIBILITY"
6. View your results

## Eligibility Requirements

- **Age**: 18-25 years old
- **Loan Amount**: P500 - P2000
- **Monthly Revenue**: Must have estimated monthly revenue projection
- **Documentation**: Must have all required documents checked
